# What's new in 1.1.0

- CAT for the FTX-1, truSDX (115200), QMX+ and Quansheng.
- Quansheng remote CAT: F+8 in SAT RX; it works on the VFO and returns to your frequency. Needs BH Satellite firmware v0.5.7.
- Phone GPS in Satellites: precise or approximate, satellite count and up to 180 s of searching.
- TLE fallback: CelesTrak, then SatNOGS, then AMSAT.
- Bridge / Diagnostics screen with "Test all".
- Improved FT2 decoder (OSD and a-priori decoding).
- Notice when the app or a radio's firmware changes.
- Signed OTA updates (GitHub build).
- New BricoHams look (brand manual v1.1).
- Samsung DeX friendly.
