Delivery conditions

  1. Review every ID and close its outstanding item with evidence, or record an approved scope change. Do not replace a requirement with a button or mock.
  2. Test software, documentation, local startup, and actual capabilities. For hardware: full backup, identification, and authorized tests.
  3. Apply and verify the orange palette when features are complete.
  4. Build installers from the same revision and verify contents, licenses, integrity, and startup. Do not reuse older packages as new ones.
  5. Publish to main, check CI and Pages, and record the published revision.
  6. Export sources, history, firmware artifacts with their sources and licenses, installers, and verifiable developmental builds. Include an inventory and hashes.
  7. Extract the ZIP to another directory, verify hashes, and test startup. Check that it contains no credentials, radio backups, or calibrations.

Export scope

The exporter creates a Beta development snapshot; it does not certify that the matrix is complete. It requires acceptance through --beta. It does not publish, build, or flash. Its use is described in Deployment. Final delivery remains conditional on the items above.

Development builds are verifiable Bricohams builds and the Git history of HEAD together with the current source state. The exporter does not include all of Documents/Multiboot by default: it contains third-party packages, configurations, and calibrations that must not be confused with a project distribution. Failed build directories are reported, not presented as firmware. The --android option includes a Beta APK delivery only if all source hashes exactly match the exported snapshot; it includes a guide, licenses, and build evidence. It does not include historical APK development builds without their corresponding sources or invent physical validation.
