Revision: 26-10-2026. Index of third-party firmware and tools studied for Karina and the Baofeng DM-32UV. The files are not published in this repository: redistribution authorization applicable to each file has not been verified. Their license cannot be inferred from being available for download. Download them from their source and verify them against these SHA-256 hashes. Brico-Hams keeps an internal copy in a private repository.

To check a file: sha256sum ARCHIVO (Linux/macOS) or Get-FileHash ARCHIVO (Windows) and compare. Explanation of each family: Karina and DM-32UV.
