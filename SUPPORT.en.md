# Support

[Español](SUPPORT.md) · [English (US)](SUPPORT.en.md) · [Français](SUPPORT.fr.md) · [Deutsch](SUPPORT.de.md)

1. Read the [help page](ayuda.en.html) (in the application: <http://127.0.0.1:8787/ayuda.en.html>): getting started, drivers, common errors, and frequently asked questions.
2. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/Brico-Hams/Radio-Web-install/issues).
3. If unresolved, open a new issue including:
   - Operating system and browser version.
   - Radio model, firmware, and cable.
   - Steps taken and exact error message.
   - Output of `npm test`.

**Do not attach** `.rwb.json` backups, calibrations, codeplugs, `RB_*` tokens, or personal identifiers: they may contain private data.

Support is voluntary and provided without warranty. Driver support is **unofficial**: drivers belong to their manufacturers. For radio firmware problems, use the [OpenGD77](https://www.opengd77.com/) or [F4HWN](https://github.com/armel/uv-k1-k5v3-firmware-custom/issues) support channels.

Vulnerabilities: follow [SECURITY.en.md](SECURITY.en.md).
