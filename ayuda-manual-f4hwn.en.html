F4HWN (FH4) manual: Bricohams menu guide

Reference manual for the F4HWN (FH4) firmware on the Quansheng UV-K5 and UV-K6: locking, backlight and the radio's 68 menus. Bricohams edition of 8-11-2026. Complete manual in PDF (13 pages; it includes the 3-page printable quick reference annex, pages 11-13). The PDF is in Spanish.

First: enable ALL

Press MENU and select ALL before using this guide. Without ALL, not all menus will appear.

  1. Press the radio's MENU key.
  2. On the selection screen, find the ALL option.
  3. Confirm ALL, then enter the general menu to see every option.

If you do not select ALL, only some of the available menus will appear, the visible numbering may look incomplete compared with this guide, and the total number of options may vary with the firmware version and build. After selecting ALL, use the guide by menu name and not only by number.

Scope and numbering

F4HWN (FH4) is a custom firmware developed by Armel, F4HWN, for the Quansheng UV-K5 family and compatible variants, including the UV-K6. It adds improvements to the interface, reception, configurable keys, scanning and power management. Which features are available depends on both the radio and the build options.

The 68 entries follow the manual's editorial order, not the universal numbering of every F4HWN version. The real list can change between builds. Always locate settings by their short name (mnemonic) if the number does not match, and check SysInf to know which build you have installed. Some of the options listed are conditional or may show different labels.

Bricohams note: the images in this web installer are F4HWN v6.1.0 for UV-K5 V3 / UV-K1 (Bricohams editions in slots). Menus are found by mnemonic and the options depend on the build.

Numbering discrepancy flagged by the manual itself: section 2.3 of the PDF numbers BLTime, BLMin and BLMax as 38, 39 and 40, while the table numbers them 36, 37 and 38. This guide follows the table.

Critical menus step by step

KeyLck (30): automatic lock

  1. Press MENU on the main screen.
  2. Find KeyLck with the navigation keys.
  3. Press MENU to edit and select OFF or a time between 00:15 and 10:00, in 15-second steps.
  4. Confirm with MENU and leave with EXIT.
  5. Wait the configured time without pressing keys and check the lock. To unlock, use the combination provided by your edition; on many variants it is a long press of F/#.

Tip: 00:30-01:00 for carrying; OFF for continuous handling. The scope of this lock depends on SetLck.

SetLck (63): lock scope

  1. Open MENU and find SetLck.
  2. Press MENU to change the setting.
  3. Select KEYS to lock only the keys, leaving PTT operational, or KEYS+PTT to lock the transmit button as well.
  4. Confirm, exit and test the behavior safely, without transmitting outside authorized frequencies.

Tip: KEYS+PTT prevents accidental transmissions while carrying; KEYS lets you use PTT without unlocking.

BLTime, BLMin, BLMax and BLTxRx: steady light

  1. Find BLTime (36 in the table) and select ON to keep the backlight on permanently. Other options: OFF or 5 seconds to 5 minutes, in 5 s steps.
  2. Enter BLMin (37): set the minimum or idle brightness, normally 0-9; start with 0-2 to save power.
  3. Enter BLMax (38): set the wake-up brightness, normally 1-10; 3-5 is a practical starting point.
  4. Check BLTxRx (39): OFF, TX, RX or TX/RX; choose which events turn on the lighting.
  5. Confirm each value and watch the screen at idle, during reception and during permitted transmission. If it turns off unexpectedly, review the minimum brightness values and the temporary-off shortcuts.

Battery life: keeping the backlight on, especially with high BLMin/BLMax, can noticeably reduce battery life. BLTime=ON with high brightness drains the battery sooner; for daily use try BLTime=10-20 s and low brightness. ON is useful on the bench, with adequate power or when visibility takes priority.

Reference for the 68 menus

Functional organization of the manual. The numbering may not match different builds; always prioritize the mnemonic.

Operating recommendations

  • Save a copy of the memories and the configuration with CHIRP before flashing, restoring or deleting channels: F4HWN CHIRP module.
  • CTCSS/DCS tones select signals: they provide no privacy, encryption or guarantee of a free channel.
  • Always respect the applicable band, power and licence limits. The technical ability to transmit outside a band is not the same as authorization.
  • If an entry changes number or is missing, check SysInf and consult the installed build before interpreting the setting.
